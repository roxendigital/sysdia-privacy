Sysdia Privacy Policy

Effective 13 October 2026

Sysdia is a blood pressure log. Your readings are stored on your phone and nowhere else. The developer cannot see them, because Sysdia has no account system, no server and no analytics, and it does not show ads.

What Sysdia stores

Everything you enter is saved in a database on your phone:

  • Readings: top and bottom numbers, pulse, the date and time, arm, body position and any note you type.
  • People: the name you give each person you track, their age group, and whether they are pregnant (used only to decide which reference ranges to show).
  • Reminders: the times and days you choose.
  • App settings, and whether Pro is unlocked.

You can track more than one person, for example a parent. Only enter information about someone else with their agreement.

What leaves your phone

Nothing leaves your phone unless you send it. Sysdia makes no network connections of its own to send your data anywhere.

  • Reports. When you choose Download report, Sysdia makes a PDF and opens your phone's share sheet. The PDF goes wherever you send it.
  • Backup files. Back up to file makes a file with all your readings. It goes wherever you save or send it. Keep it private, because anyone who has the file can read it.
  • Support email. If you choose Contact support, your email app opens with a message to the address below. Sysdia adds nothing except the subject line.

Phone backups

Your phone's own backup system may copy Sysdia's data. On Android that is your Google backup, and on iPhone it is iCloud, if you have them turned on. Those copies are stored by Google or Apple under their own terms, and you control them in your phone's settings. Sysdia does not read or manage them.

Purchases

Sysdia Pro is a one-time purchase made through Google Play. Google handles the payment and keeps its own records under the Google Play terms. Sysdia does not see your card or payment details. The app keeps only a record of whether Pro is unlocked, so it can restore it if you reinstall.

Notifications and permissions

Sysdia asks for permission to show notifications so it can remind you to take a reading. Reminders are created on your phone and need no internet connection. Sysdia does not ask for your location, contacts, camera, microphone or photos.

Who Sysdia is for

Sysdia is written for adults. Reference ranges are shown only for adults, and not during pregnancy. Sysdia is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from them. Because nothing is sent to the developer, there is no child data to collect.

Not medical advice

Sysdia is a record-keeping tool. It does not diagnose or treat anything, and it does not give medical advice. Talk to your health care provider about your readings. If you feel unwell or are worried about a reading, get medical help.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays on your phone until you remove it. You can delete readings inside the app, or delete everything by uninstalling Sysdia. Backup files and phone backups you made are separate copies, so delete those as well if you want everything gone. The developer holds no copy, so there is nothing to ask the developer to delete.

Changes to this policy

If Sysdia changes how it handles information, for example by adding cloud sync, this page will be updated first and the effective date above will change. Cloud sync is not planned, and it would be optional.
